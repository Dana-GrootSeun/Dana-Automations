Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This notice explains how I handle personal information when you use this website or contact me, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Dana Theron, trading as Dana Automates, a sole freelancer based in South Africa, is the responsible party. Contact: dana.automates@gmail.com.

What I collect

If you send an enquiry through the form, I collect:

  • your name, email address, the service you're asking about and the details you write;
  • your IP address, used only to stop spam and repeated submissions;
  • the date and time of your enquiry.

If you email me or message me on WhatsApp, I receive whatever you include in your message. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if we agree, do the work — this is a step you asked for.
  • To protect the form from spam and abuse — a legitimate interest that doesn't override your rights.

I don't sell your information, use it for marketing lists, or make automated decisions about you.

How long I keep it

Security

Form submissions travel over an encrypted connection and are stored in a database that the public cannot read. Only I can access it.
