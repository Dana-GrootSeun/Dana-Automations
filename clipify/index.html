A typical night

No uploads, no editing app, no command prompt. These are the times for a six-hour Saturday stream on the Creator plan; a longer stream just finishes later in the night.

  1. 23:40 · You go offline

    End the stream as normal. There is nothing to upload: your channel was linked once, at setup.

  2. 01:12 · Your VOD is collected

    The recording comes straight from YouTube, Twitch or Kick, or from a file you dropped in your shared folder.

    saturday-stream.mp45:42:10 · 6.2 GB

  3. 02:30 · Moments found

    The whole stream is transcribed and scanned. Chat spikes and audio peaks mark the candidates; each one is checked for a hook and a payoff.

    saturday-stream.mp43 moments
    Chat reactionClutch playBig pull

  4. 04:10 · Clips cut and finished

    Each moment is cut tight, reframed to 9:16, captioned in your style and levelled for phone speakers.

    • Cut tight
    • Reframe 9:16
    • Captions
    • Loudness

  5. 06:00 · Every clip checked

    I watch each clip in full. Weak ones are dropped rather than padded out, and each one that stays gets its title, description and hashtags.

  6. 07:00 · Ready to post

    The batch lands in your shared folder with a posting order. Open it on your phone and post.

Framed for your setup

Any stream with a saved VOD: YouTube Live, Twitch or Kick, in English. The layout is mapped per channel at setup.

  • Face cam stacked over the fight, cropped around the action rather than the centre of the screen.

  • Your camera fills the frame and the chat message you are reacting to is set on screen.

  • The vertical frame follows you through the shot instead of sitting in the middle of the street.

  • The pack cam takes the top two thirds so the pull is readable; your reaction sits underneath.

  • Gameplay with your cam and the run timer kept in frame, so the clip shows what the run was.

  • Host and guest stacked, switching to whoever is speaking.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Clipify runs on my side. You stream as normal, and clips arrive in a shared folder you can open on your phone.

Do you post the clips for me?

By default you post them, using the titles and hashtags I supply. If you'd rather I upload them, give me Editor access to your YouTube channel and we'll agree a schedule.

What if I don't like a clip?

Tell me which one and why. I'll re-cut it or swap it, and save the note to your preferences so the next batch gets it right.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month: tell me before your next billing date and that month is your last.

Which platforms and games work?

Any stream with a saved VOD or recording: YouTube, Twitch or Kick, in English. Games, IRL, pack openings and just chatting all work; the layout is set up per channel.

You sleep. I clip.

Tell me where you stream and how often. Within 24 hours you'll get a reply with a start date.
