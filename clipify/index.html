Done-for-you stream clipping

One stream in.
A week of clips out.

Your 3–8 hour stream becomes captioned vertical clips for Shorts, TikTok and Reels. Every clip is checked by a person before it reaches you.

Why Clipify exists

You already made the content. The slow part is finding the moments, reframing them, fixing captions and packaging the batch.

From VOD to folder

Four steps from stream to folder.

  1. 01

    Share the recording

    I collect the saved VOD from your channel, or you drop the file into a shared folder.

  2. 02

    Find the moments

    The stream is transcribed and cut around moments with a clear hook and payoff.

  3. 03

    Finish every clip

    Each cut is reframed to 9:16, captioned in your style and checked for audio and timing.

  4. 04

    Open your folder

    You get ready-to-post files, titles, descriptions, hashtags and a suggested order.

What arrives

Not a pile of rough cuts.

A complete batch, organised so you can post from your phone without reopening an editor.
